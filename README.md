# HR Analytics Dashboard: Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to understand the factors influencing employee attrition and identify trends that affect workforce retention.

The project follows a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow using Excel, SQL, and Power BI. It covers data cleaning, business analysis, dashboard development, and actionable business recommendations.

The final dashboard provides interactive insights into employee demographics, attrition trends, satisfaction levels, compensation, and career progression.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and affects overall business performance.

This project aims to answer the following business questions:

- Which employees are leaving the company?
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which job roles experience the most turnover?
- Does salary influence employee retention?
- Does overtime contribute to employee attrition?
- Are employee satisfaction levels linked to attrition?

---

The dataset contains information for **1,470 employees**.

---

## Key Insights

- Overall employee attrition rate was **16.12%**.
- Employees aged **26–35** recorded the highest turnover.
- Some departments and job roles experienced significantly higher attrition than others.
- Employees working overtime were more likely to leave the company.
- Lower salary categories accounted for a larger number of employee exits.
- Employee satisfaction and career growth showed a strong relationship with employee retention.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Review compensation strategies for lower-paid employees.
- Improve employee career development opportunities.
- Monitor departments with consistently high attrition.
- Reduce excessive overtime to prevent employee burnout.
- Improve employee engagement and satisfaction programs.
- Use HR analytics regularly to identify and address retention risks.
